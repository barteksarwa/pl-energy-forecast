\section{Walk-forward validation}

\subsection{Why normal cross-validation is forbidden here}

Standard $k$-fold CV shuffles data randomly. On time series that trains on
Friday to predict the previous Monday. Two failures:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Leakage.} Load is autocorrelated; a training hour 3 hours
        after a test hour carries most of its information. Scores inflate.
  \item \textbf{Wrong question.} Production always predicts \emph{forward}.
        Only a forward-in-time evaluation estimates production error.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{How walk-forward works}

Mimic the desk, day by day:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Pick a test day $D$.
  \item Train on a trailing window that ends before $D$
        (ours: 365 days, so the model always sees every season once).
  \item Forecast all hours of $D$ under the 09:00 D-1 cutoff.
  \item Advance one day. Repeat over the whole test period.
\end{enumerate}

Every test day is scored by a model that knew nothing about it or anything
after it. Our engine: \texttt{src/evaluation/backtest.py}.

\subsection{Two engineering choices worth defending}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Refit every 7 days, not daily.} Refitting LASSO daily over a
        year of test days is slow and changes little. Between refits, the last
        model keeps forecasting --- exactly how desks run models.
  \item \textbf{Rolling window, not expanding.} A fixed 365-day window forgets
        old regimes (fuel-cost shifts, post-2022 demand changes). An expanding
        window remembers everything, including what no longer applies.
        Debatable both ways; we test the choice in M3 rather than argue it.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Proof, not promise}

Test in \texttt{tests/test\_backtest.py}: corrupt the target with garbage
($10^9$) everywhere after the test start; predictions must not change.
If training ever touched the future, they would.

\subsection{Interview line}

``My backtest walks forward like the desk lives: trailing training window,
weekly refits, the 09:00 cutoff enforced every single day. And I prove no
future data leaks by corrupting it and showing nothing changes.''
