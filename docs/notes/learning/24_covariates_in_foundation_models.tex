\section{Covariates in foundation models --- why zero-shot cannot use them}

\subsection{The question}

Our champion's skill comes almost entirely from covariates:
tomorrow's wind, solar and load forecasts. Zero-shot foundation
models are univariate --- they see only price history.
Moirai (Salesforce) claims native covariate support.
So we tested: does handing a zero-shot model our covariates help?

\subsection{The experiment}

Two Moirai variants, identical everywhere else:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{moirai\_zs} --- univariate, price history only.
\item \texttt{moirai\_cov} --- price history + RES forecast + TSO
      load forecast as known-future covariates.
\end{itemize}
Their delta is the covariate lift, measured cleanly.
Same 2-year backtest as every other model.

\subsection{The result: covariates HURT}

\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\hline
Model & MAE & rMAE & Coverage \\
\hline
Moirai univariate & 23.69 & 0.849 & 77.0\% \\
Moirai + covariates & 24.86 & 0.890 & 75.5\% \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5em}
Adding the covariates made it 1.2 EUR/MWh WORSE.
The difference is DM-significant. Not noise.

\subsection{Why}

A trained model learns the mapping from OUR covariates to OUR
prices: 1 GW more wind $\approx$ X EUR less. Moirai learned
covariate handling on other domains during pretraining.
At inference it has no way to learn what a megawatt of Polish wind
is worth. The covariate channel adds inputs without adding the
mapping --- extra dimensions, zero domain knowledge. Noise in,
noise out.

Contrast with the champion: the SAME covariates carry 96\% of its
skill. The information is fine. Using it needs training.

\subsection{The general lesson}

Zero-shot transfers \emph{patterns} (cycles, spikes, mean
reversion). It cannot transfer \emph{relationships} between your
specific inputs and your target. Whenever covariates matter ---
and in energy prices they dominate --- some form of fitting on
local data is mandatory. This is the strongest argument in the
repo for why energy desks still train their own models.

\subsection{Interview line}

``I tested Moirai's covariate mode zero-shot: it was DM-significantly
worse than univariate Moirai. Covariate skill is a learned mapping,
not an architectural feature. Same covariates carry 96\% of my
trained champion's skill --- the information is there, but a
zero-shot model can't price it.''
