\section{Zero-shot foundation models --- forecasting without training}

\subsection{The idea}

A foundation model is a large model pretrained on huge, varied data.
For text: GPT. For time series: Chronos (Amazon, 2024).
Chronos treats a time series like a sentence. Values are scaled and
binned into a fixed vocabulary of tokens. A transformer then learns
``next token prediction'' --- exactly like a language model.
Pretraining data: billions of observations from many domains
(energy, traffic, retail, weather, synthetic series).

\subsection{Zero-shot means zero training on our data}

We never fit the model. We hand it a window of recent PL prices.
It returns sample paths for the next day. Quantiles come from the
samples, so probabilistic output is free. No GPU training runs,
no hyperparameter search, no seeds. One forward pass per forecast.

The bet: patterns learned from millions of other series transfer
to ours. Daily cycles, weekly cycles, spikes, mean reversion ---
Chronos has seen them all somewhere.

\subsection{The catch: univariate}

Chronos sees only the price history. Nothing else.
Our champion also sees wind and solar forecasts (RES), the TSO load
forecast, and calendar features. For PL prices these covariates
matter a lot: tomorrow's wind forecast moves tomorrow's price.
So the comparison is deliberately unfair to Chronos.
The gap measures what covariates plus training buy.

\subsection{Worked example: our numbers}

Standard 2-year backtest, daily context refresh. rMAE is MAE
relative to seasonal naive (lower is better, naive $=$ 1.000).

\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\hline
Model & MAE (EUR/MWh) & rMAE \\
\hline
Champion LGBM (covariates, trained) & 17.87 & 0.640 \\
TFT-730 ens-3 (covariates, trained) & 19.52 & 0.699 \\
Chronos-Bolt zero-shot (univariate) & 21.98 & 0.787 \\
PatchTST-730 ens-3 (covariates, trained) & 22.25 & 0.797 \\
Seasonal naive & 27.91 & 1.000 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5em}
Read the table twice. Chronos, with no training and no covariates,
beats our trained PatchTST. But it loses clearly to TFT and to the
champion. Foundation models are a strong free baseline here,
not a champion.

\subsection{Interview line}

``I benchmarked Chronos-Bolt zero-shot on PL prices: rMAE 0.787.
It beat our trained PatchTST without a single gradient step ---
but covariates plus training were still worth 4 EUR/MWh of MAE.
That gap is why energy desks still train their own models.''
