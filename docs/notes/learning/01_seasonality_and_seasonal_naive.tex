\section{Seasonality and the seasonal naive model}

\subsection{What seasonality is}

A repeating pattern with a fixed period. Electricity load has three at once:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Daily.} Night valley, morning ramp, evening peak. Period: 24 hours.
  \item \textbf{Weekly.} Weekdays high, weekends low. Monday morning differs from
        Saturday morning. Period: 7 days.
  \item \textbf{Yearly.} Winter heating and dark hours push load up. Summer cooling
        adds a smaller bump. Period: 365 days.
\end{itemize}

Key insight: the strongest predictor of load at hour $H$ is load at hour $H$ in a
past period.

\subsection{The seasonal naive model}

Forecast = the value one season ago. Nothing else:
\begin{equation}
  \hat{y}_{t} = y_{t - m},
\end{equation}
where $m$ is the season length. Our choice: $m = 168$ hours (7 days).

Why 7 days and not 1? A 1-day lag would predict Monday from Sunday. Wrong regime.
The 7-day lag keeps the weekday pattern. It handles daily and weekly seasonality
for free.

\subsection{Worked example}

Last Tuesday 18:00 the load was 21{,}400~MW. Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Seasonal naive forecast for 18:00: 21{,}400~MW. Done.

For uncertainty we take the last 4 same-weekday values at 18:00:
21{,}400 / 20{,}900 / 22{,}100 / 21{,}000~MW.
Empirical quantiles: P10 $\approx$ 20{,}930~MW, P90 $\approx$ 21{,}890~MW.
The spread of recent history becomes the uncertainty band.

\subsection{When it fails}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Holidays. Next Tuesday is a holiday, last Tuesday was not.
  \item Weather swings. A cold snap raises load; last week was mild.
  \item Trends. Slow economic or efficiency changes leak in over months.
\end{itemize}

Every fancy model must beat this first. In forecasting, many do not.
That is why it is the mandatory baseline (\texttt{docs/PLAN.md}, M3).

\subsection{Interview line}

``My headline metric is skill over seasonal naive --- how much error the model
removes relative to just copying last week. If a deep net has zero skill,
it is not learning anything the calendar did not already know.''
