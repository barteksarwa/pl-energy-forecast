\section{Deep-model gap decomposition: how much was our fault?}

The attention campaign ended with a question. Deep models lost to
LightGBM on PL day-ahead price. Was the architecture wrong? Or was our
own evaluation setup handicapping them?

We answered it by decomposition. Fix one suspect at a time. Measure the
MAE gain. Everything below runs on one common setup: 1-year test window
(2025-07-16$\to$, 8,760~h), walk-forward, monthly refits. Champion:
LGBM at 17.66 EUR/MWh.

\subsection{The ledger}

Worked example: walk the PatchTST chain step by step.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
step & MAE (EUR/MWh) & gained \\
\midrule
start: 365 d window, 1 seed, d\_model 64 & 21.50 & --- \\
training window $\to$ 730 d & 20.27 & $+1.2$ \\
median ensemble of 3 seeds & 19.94 & $+0.3$ \\
capacity d\_model $\to$ 128 & 19.78 & $+0.2$ \\
swap architecture $\to$ TFT-730 ens-3 & 18.31 & $+1.5$ \\
champion LGBM & 17.66 & 0.65 left \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Read it in two halves. Window, ensemble, capacity are \emph{fixable
handicaps} --- our setup, not the model. Together: $+1.7$. The
architecture swap is \emph{structural}: $+1.5$. TFT's LSTM +
variable-selection path beats patch attention on this task. The final
0.65 (3.7\%) is the honest remaining gap. TFT-730 ens-3 also hits
82.8\% coverage --- above the 80\% target, no conformal wrap.
PatchTST-730 ens-3 stalls at 19.94 (19.78 with the d128 capacity fix).

Headline framing, same-window numbers only: the PatchTST-365 gap was
3.84 EUR/MWh. Setup levers recovered 1.7. The architecture swap
recovered 1.5. Nearly half the ``deep learning loses'' verdict was our
evaluation setup, not the models.

\subsection{What did not help}

Hyperparameter re-tuning at 730 d gave nothing. The seed-42
``winner'' (dropout 0.30, MAE 17.97) failed confirmation on seeds
7 and 2026: 20.17 and 19.43. Its ensemble matched the baseline. This
is the \emph{third} time this project that a 1-seed screen picked a
mirage. Rule: never promote a config from one seed.

\subsection{Ablation verdicts are conditional}

At 365 d windows, removing PatchTST's price-history encoder
\emph{helped} ($-0.4$ EUR/MWh). At 730 d it \emph{hurt} ($+2.5$,
all 3 seeds). Same model, same input, opposite verdict. An ablation
answers ``is this input useful \emph{under this training config}?'' ---
not ``is it useful, full stop''. Stress-test the verdict against the
config knob it depends on most.

\subsection{Interview lines}

``Before blaming the architecture, I priced every handicap in my own
evaluation setup. On a common test window the decomposition was:
training window $+1.2$, seed ensemble $+0.3$, capacity $+0.2$ ---
all self-inflicted --- versus $+1.5$ for the architecture choice
itself. That cut the deep-model gap to 0.65 EUR/MWh and told me the
rest was structural, so I stopped tuning and kept LightGBM.''

``A single-seed hyperparameter winner beat the champion by 0.3 ---
then lost by 2 on the confirmation seeds. Third mirage like that in
one project. I now confirm every screen on multiple seeds before
believing it.''
