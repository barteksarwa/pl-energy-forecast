\section{P\&L verdict --- does MAE rank survive contact with money?}

\subsection{The question}

PLAN Phase 7 flagged the risk: MAE rank and capture rank can
diverge. A model with better average error but worse peak/trough
timing would earn less. Did that happen?

\subsection{Result: ranks match, gaps compress}

2-year battery backtest (1 MW / 2 MWh / 0.85 RTE / 1 cycle,
same 713 days for every model):

\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\hline
Model & MAE & MAE rank & Capture & EUR/day \\
\hline
Ensemble (CRPS+CQR) & 17.34 & 1 & 0.924 & 205 \\
LGBM champion & 17.87 & 2 & 0.914 & 202 \\
LEAR & 18.24 & 3 & 0.908 & 201 \\
Chronos & 21.99 & 4 & 0.890 & 197 \\
TimesFM & 22.52 & 5 & 0.880 & 195 \\
Naive & 27.96 & 6 & 0.813 & 180 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5em}
No rank flip anywhere. MAE was a faithful proxy for storage value
on this market and period. The honest caveat: gaps compress hard
(learning note 25) --- 10.6 MAE between naive and ensemble is worth
only 11 capture points.

\subsection{What the ensemble is worth in money}

Ensemble over champion: $+2.3$ EUR/day on a 1 MW battery,
$\approx 850$ EUR/year. Real but small. Scale it: a 100 MW
portfolio makes that 85k EUR/year --- the ensemble pays for its
operational complexity at portfolio scale, not on one battery.
This framing is the right answer to ``why bother with 0.5 MAE?''

\subsection{Loss days}

Perfect foresight never loses money (it can sit idle).
Forecast schedules can: the ensemble lost money on 1.7\% of days,
naive on 4.5\%. Loss days cluster on spike days the schedule
mistimed --- consistent with every spike finding in this repo.

\subsection{Verdict}

\begin{itemize}
\item The ensemble is the best earner. Its promotion case now has
      a EUR number attached.
\item No MAE-vs-capture rank divergence to report. Watch item
      closed.
\item Storage P\&L is a compressed lens. Keep both: MAE for model
      development, EUR for stakeholder communication.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Interview line}

``I checked whether error rank survives translation into battery
P\&L. It did --- no rank flips --- but the spread compressed from
10.6 MAE to 11 points of capture. My ensemble's 0.5 MAE edge is
about 850 EUR/year per MW of storage. That is how I would justify
a model upgrade to a desk: in EUR per megawatt, not in MAE.''
