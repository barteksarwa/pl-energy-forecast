\section{4-member blend verdict --- TFT the diversity donor}

\subsection{What was tested}

A 4-member CRPS-weighted blend. Members: champion LGBM+CQR,
LEAR+CQR, Chronos+CQR, and TFT-730 ens-3+CQR.
Same inverse trailing-60-day crps3 weights as the 3-member blend
(learning note 23). A second rolling-CQR pass tightens the band.
TFT is the new fourth member. Its full 2-year predictions had
survived the data wipe under \texttt{reports/sensitivity/tft/}.
So the deferred ``1-year with TFT'' test became a clean 2-year test.

Evaluated on the 17,456-hour intersection (TFT window ends 2026-07-14).

\subsection{TFT the diversity donor}

This is the interesting part. TFT lost solo.
It was archived on 2026-07-21: 19.53 MAE, worse than the champion.
Yet it is the best diversity donor we have tested.
Deep-model errors decorrelate from trees, linear, and the FM.
So TFT adds the one thing the other three lack: a fourth failure mode.
The blend beats its best member by more once TFT joins.
A weak member can still be the most valuable member.
Strength solo and value in a blend are different things.

\subsection{The TimesFM counter-example}

We also tried TimesFM as a fourth member instead of TFT.
It added nothing: $+0.57$ MAE, a loss.
TimesFM is a foundation model, like Chronos.
Its errors correlate with Chronos's errors --- same model family.
So it brought no new failure mode.
Lesson: diversity of \emph{error structure} matters.
Not member count. Not member strength.
Adding a fourth model helps only if it fails differently.

\subsection{Verdict}

\textbf{\texttt{ens4\_tft} is the promotion candidate for the price
product.} MAE 16.89, rMAE 0.605, coverage 80.0\%, Winkler 82.6,
P\&L capture 0.929. It replaces the 3-member blend as the candidate.
Every gate was smashed, not just cleared.

\subsection{Honest caveats}

\begin{itemize}
\item Promotion is an owner call. The blocker is operational cost.
\item A 3-seed deep model in the daily loop is expensive:
      MPS inference plus monthly refits, roughly hours per month.
\item Evaluated on the 17,456-hour intersection, not the full window
      --- TFT's stored predictions end 2026-07-14.
\item These tables predate the corrected-cutoff protocol
      (E2 fix). Every trained member carries the same $\sim$0.11 MAE
      of shared flattery; rankings stand.
\end{itemize}
