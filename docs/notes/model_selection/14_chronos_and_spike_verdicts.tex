\section{Phase 5 verdicts --- Chronos zero-shot and the spike classifier}

\subsection{Verdict 1: Chronos-Bolt zero-shot --- strong baseline, gate closed}

Setup: standard 2-year backtest, daily context refresh, CQR on top
for bands. No training on our data. Univariate: price history only.

\begin{tabular}{lccl}
\hline
Model & MAE & rMAE & Verdict \\
\hline
Champion LGBM (365d) & 17.87 & 0.640 & champion \\
TFT-730 ens-3 & 19.52 & 0.699 & best deep \\
Chronos-Bolt zero-shot & 21.98 & 0.787 & benchmark row \\
PatchTST-730 ens-3 (trained) & 22.25 & 0.797 & beaten \\
Naive & 27.91 & 1.000 & floor \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.5em}
Fairness footnote, always attached: Chronos is covariate-blind.
The champion sees RES, TSO forecast, and calendar. The gap measures
what covariates plus training buy, not raw model quality.

The story that matters: zero training beats our trained PatchTST
(22.25, DM-significant). Months of patch-attention work, matched by
a downloaded checkpoint. Humbling and useful.

The gate: Phase 6 fine-tuning required rMAE $< 0.75$. Result: 0.787.
Gate closed. Fine-tuning judgment is deferred, with this number in
hand. Coverage with CQR: 79.9\% --- the bands were honest for free.

\subsection{Verdict 2: spike classifier --- promoted}

Three strikes led here. Asymmetric CQR: no gain. GPD tail: no gain.
Christoffersen test: band violations cluster on consecutive hours.
All three said the same thing: spike misses are \emph{conditional}.
The model lacks information, not band width. So stop widening bands.
Predict the spike directly, from features that see it coming
(tight reserve margin, low wind, high load).

Setup: 2-year walk-forward, spike $=$ top-5\% price hours,
labels from the training window only. Gate: AUC $\geq 0.80$.

Result: AUC 0.966, Brier 0.034, precision@2 0.736. Gate passed.
Promoted: the daily report now carries a $p_\text{spike}$ column
per hour.

The seed lesson. Plan said: confirm with 3 seeds. But the classifier
is deterministic --- no subsampling in its parameters. Seeds 42 and 7
produced bit-identical results. The sweep was vacuous, so we stopped
it. Know when a robustness check checks nothing.
